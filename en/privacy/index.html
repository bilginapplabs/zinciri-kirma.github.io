In short

  • Your habits, notes and settings stay only on your phone; they are not sent to any server.
  • The App does not ask you to create an account or to give an email address or phone number. There are no ads.
  • Optional: If you allow it, anonymous usage statistics and crash reports are sent to Google's Firebase service. If you do not allow it, or turn it off later, nothing is sent.
  • Optional Zincir+ purchases are made through Google Play; we cannot see your payment details.
  • Optional (Zincir+): If you turn on the automatic link for a habit, the steps, sleep, exercise or water data you pick is read from Health Connect only on your phone; no health data is stored, backed up or sent.
  • You can delete all of it at any time with Profile › Settings › "Delete all my data".

Why does this text exist, and who is responsible?

Zinciri Kırma ("the App") is developed by Erhan Bilgin ("the Developer"), an individual developer publishing under the name Bilgin Apps. This text has been prepared for the duty to inform under Article 10 of the Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu, "KVKK") and for the app stores' requirement of a privacy policy.

The Developer cannot access the data you keep on your phone; the Developer does not collect it and does not transfer it to anyone. Only the anonymous usage and crash data that you allow reaches the Developer's Firebase account, as described below.

Data kept on your phone

  • The name, frequency, time, reminder setting and reminder times of the habits and tasks you create, and their Health Connect target if any (for example "8,000 steps a day"; only the target, not the data read).
  • Which days you completed, the notes you wrote for a completion, and the days you marked as rest days.
  • The name shown on your profile. As a guest it reads "Guest"; you can change it as you like.
  • App settings such as your theme preference, quiet hours and daily summary settings, whether you have seen the introduction screen, the time of the last backup, and the permissions you have granted.

What you write in a habit name or a note is entirely up to you. If you write sensitive information, such as information about your health, it is likewise kept only on your device; you are not required to write it. We recommend that you set a screen lock on your phone; on a phone without a screen lock or on a rooted phone, the protection is reduced.

Optional: anonymous usage and crash data

When you first open the App, you are asked whether you want to share this. If your answer is "Don't share", or if you have not answered at all, nothing is sent. You can change your decision at any time with the switch in Profile › Settings › Privacy and data.

What is sent if you allow it:

  • General events: What Firebase collects by itself: the App being opened, time of use, updates and Google Play purchases (product, price, currency).
  • Screens: Which screens are opened (for example Today, Stats, Settings).
  • Habits: Creating, editing, archiving, pinning, reordering and deleting. Only the repeat type (for example "daily"), the goal type, whether it has a category, the number of reminders, the data type if it is connected to Health Connect (for example "steps"; never a value) and, when deleted, how many days it existed (as a range).
  • Days: Ticking or unticking a link, rest days and writing a note (not the note itself). Only whether it was today or an earlier day, how it was marked (by tapping, with an amount, with a note) and the streak reaching a step such as 7, 30 or 100.
  • Features: The category filter (only whether a built-in or your own category), starting a ready-made program (which one), creating and deleting categories, making and restoring a backup (how many habits, as a range), sharing a story (which template), adding or removing a widget, a reminder being shown and the "Done" and "Snooze 10 min" buttons on it.
  • Zincir+: Where the plans screen was opened from, which locked feature was tapped, the plan picked, a purchase starting and how it ended (completed, pending, cancelled, failed), restoring purchases, your answer to the trial notices, and tips.
  • Settings: The value picked when the theme, colour, app icon, first day of the week or language changes; whether quiet hours and the daily summary are on or off.
  • Crash report: the record of the error, the App version, the phone model and the Android version.
  • Firebase's random identifier specific to the App, approximate location (Google may derive it from connection information), and diagnostic information at the moment of the crash.

These events consist only of fixed choices, yes/no answers and numbers rounded into ranges; they contain no text you wrote and no dates.

Never sent: your habit and category names, your notes, the emojis you pick, your profile name, your email address and phone number, the dates of your days, the numbers of your goals, any value read from Health Connect, your advertising ID.

Who processes it: The data is processed by Google's Firebase service (Analytics and Crashlytics); this data may be processed on servers outside Türkiye (including in the USA). The Developer has access only to aggregated statistics and crash records. The purpose is to find bugs and improve the App; the data is not used for advertising.

Legal ground and transfer abroad: This processing is based on your explicit consent and starts only when you tap "Share". The transfer of your data to Google's servers outside Türkiye is subject to Article 9 of the KVKK; the Developer is obliged to carry out this transfer in accordance with the Firebase data processing terms agreed with Google and the safeguards required by the legislation in force.

Withdrawal: When you turn the switch off, no new data is sent, the analytics identifier on the phone is reset, and crash reports that have not yet been sent are deleted. Data already sent, which does not carry your identity, may remain with Google for Google's retention periods.

Other ways data is shared

The two other ways your data can leave your phone are also under your control:

  • Android Auto Backup: If Google backup is turned on on your phone, Android may back up the App's data to your Google account (once a day, while charging and on Wi-Fi) and restore it on a new phone. This is Google's service and Google's terms apply; the Developer cannot access this backup. To turn it off, see Settings › Google › Backup on your phone.
  • File backup: When you tap Profile › Settings › Backup › "Back up now", a file containing your habits and their history is created and you save it to a place you choose (for example Google Drive). This file is not encrypted; it is readable text. Keep it in a safe place and do not share it with others.

Notifications and the widgets you add to your home screen show the names of your habits and your streak on your phone's screen. They do not leave the phone, but anyone who can see your screen can see them too. If you wish, you can turn off the notification for each habit and remove a widget from the home screen.

Zincir+ purchases

Zincir+ (the advanced features) is optional. If you buy it, Google Play takes and processes the payment; your card or payment details never reach the Developer. This is subject to Google's terms and privacy policy.

  • On your phone: The App asks Google Play, on your phone, what you have bought (the product, the purchase state and the purchase token Google issues) and acknowledges the purchase. The day your free trial started and the purchase state Google Play last reported are kept on your phone; they are not part of backups and are not sent to any server.
  • If you allowed analytics: The anonymous Zincir+ events described above (such as the plan picked and how a purchase ended) and Firebase's Google Play purchase event are sent as well; the purchase token and the order number are not.
  • What the Developer sees: Google Play Console shows the seller order information for sales (such as the order number, product, price, country, date and refund status). The Developer uses it only to resolve problems with purchases, handle refunds and meet legal obligations.
  • Legal ground: This processing is necessary to conclude and perform the contract for the service you bought (KVKK Article 5(2)(c)) and for the Developer's legal obligations (KVKK Article 5(2)(ç)). Order information is kept in Google Play for the period the relevant legislation requires.

Optional: automatic links from Health Connect

With Zincir+ you can connect a habit to Health Connect: once the day's total reaches the target, the link is added by itself. This feature is off by default; it works only if you turn it on for a habit yourself and grant the permission in Health Connect.

  • What is read: Only the daily total of the one data type you picked for that habit: the number of steps, the sleep time (the night that ends that morning), the exercise time or the amount of water drunk. It is read for today and yesterday.
  • How it is used: The total read is used to see whether the day reached the target and to show today's progress (for example "6,240 / 8,000 steps") on the Today screen and the habit's page; it is held only in the phone's memory while the App runs and is gone when the App closes. No health data is written to the App's database or settings, put into backups or exported files, sent to the internet or to the Developer, or added to analytics events. The App writes nothing to Health Connect.
  • What is kept: The habit's setting (which data type and the target) and the days completed automatically; a completed day becomes part of the habit's history, like a day you tick by hand. Which days of the last two were completed automatically is also kept on this phone only and is not part of backups, so that a day you untick by hand is not ticked again.
  • Analytics: If you allowed analytics, habit events say only which data type a habit is connected to (for example "steps"); your target, the values read and the days completed automatically are not sent.
  • In the background: If Health Connect allows reading in the background, the App checks once an hour, also while it is closed. If you do not grant this, the check runs only when you open the App.
  • Permission and withdrawal: Health Connect asks for each data type on its own screen. You can take the permission back at any time in Health Connect or in Android's settings, and turn the feature off in the habit's edit screen; from then on nothing is read.

Legal ground: Health data is special category personal data under Article 6 of the KVKK. This reading is based only on your explicit consent: you turn the feature on for a habit yourself and you grant the permission in Health Connect. Since the data does not leave your phone, the Developer cannot access it and there is no transfer abroad. Data read from Health Connect is used only for this feature, in line with Google Play's Health Connect policy; it is not used for ads, sold or used for any other purpose.

The automatic link is not a medical measurement or advice; it relies on data that other apps and devices write to Health Connect.

Permissions requested

  • Notifications: To show reminders and, if you turned it on, the daily summary.
  • Exact alarms: So that a notification arrives at exactly the right time. If you do not grant it, notifications may be delayed by up to an hour.
  • Running when the phone starts: To set up reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Google Play purchases: To make and restore Zincir+ purchases through Google Play.
  • Health Connect reading (steps, sleep, exercise, water, reading in the background): Asked only for a habit you turn the automatic link on for, and only for the data type you picked; see the Health Connect section above.
  • Internet access: Used only to send the optional data described above, if you have allowed it. Your habits are never sent to the internet.

The App does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or advertising ID permissions. File backup uses Android's own file picker.

Purpose of processing and legal ground

The data on your phone is processed only on your device, for the purpose of providing you with habit tracking, reminders, statistics and backups; since the Developer does not access it, explicit consent is not requested for it. Anonymous usage and crash data, on the other hand, is processed only with your explicit consent. Health data read from Health Connect is likewise processed only with your explicit consent, only on your device and only for the automatic link. If a feature that collects other data, such as accounts, cloud sync or sharing, is added in the future, this text will be updated; where necessary, you will be informed in advance and asked for your approval.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data read from Health Connect is not kept at all. With Profile › Settings › "Delete all my data" you delete all your habits, their history and your settings; this also resets your answer to the sharing question. The Google backup and the backup files you saved yourself stay in your account or in the place you chose; you need to delete them separately.

Exercising your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you can exercise rights such as learning whether your data is processed, correction, deletion, withdrawing your consent and transferring your data directly within the App: you correct data by editing your habit, delete it with "Delete all my data", withdraw your consent by turning the switch off, and move your data with the backup file. You can also write to bilginapplabs@gmail.com. Your right to apply and complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu) is reserved.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
