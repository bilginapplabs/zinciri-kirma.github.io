Zincir+, the trial and payments

  • Free trial: A 30-day trial starts on the day you first open the App; Zincir+ features are open during it. No payment details are asked for and the trial never turns into a paid subscription by itself. When it ends, the advanced features close; your habits, history and settings are not deleted and the basic features stay free.

A subscription renews automatically at the end of each period for the same length and price unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends; the renewal is charged to your Apple Account within the 24 hours before the period ends.

  • Scope: The features Zincir+ includes are listed in the App. We will not narrow what you bought to your disadvantage during a period you paid for.
  • Tips: An optional, one-time support for the Developer; it opens or closes no feature and does not replace Zincir+. Its payment and refunds go through the App Store under Apple's rules, like Zincir+.
