In short

  • Your habits, notes and settings stay only on your device; they are never sent to a server.
  • No account, email address or phone number is asked for. There are no ads.
  • Optional: If you allow it, anonymous usage statistics and crash reports are sent to Google's Firebase service. If you do not, or turn it off later, nothing is sent. There are no ads and no tracking.
  • Optional Zincir+ purchases are made through the App Store; we cannot see your payment details.
  • Optional (Zincir+): If you turn on the automatic link for a habit, the steps, sleep, exercise or water data you pick is read from Apple Health on your device only; no health data is stored, backed up or sent.
  • You can delete everything at any time with Profile › Settings › "Delete all my data".

Why this text exists, and who is responsible

Zinciri Kırma ("the App") is developed by Erhan Bilgin, an individual developer publishing as Bilgin Apps ("the Developer"). This text is the notice required by Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the privacy policy the App Store requires, for the iPhone and iPad version of the app. The Android version has its own text.

The Developer cannot access the data kept on your device; it does not collect it or pass it to anyone.

Data kept on your device

  • The name, schedule, time, reminder settings and times of the habits and tasks you create, and an Apple Health goal if you set one (for example "8,000 steps a day"; only the goal, never the data read).
  • Which days you completed, the notes you wrote for them and the days you marked as rest days.
  • The name shown on your profile. As a guest it reads "Guest User"; you can change it.
  • App settings such as your theme, quiet hours and daily summary, whether you have seen the introduction, when you last backed up, and the permissions you gave.

This data is kept in a folder on this device that the app and its widgets share (an app group). What you write in a habit's name or a note is up to you. If you write sensitive information, such as about your health, it too stays only on your device; you do not have to write it. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode or Face ID / Touch ID.

Optional: anonymous usage and crash data

When you first open the app, you are asked whether you want to share this. If your answer is "Don't share", or if you have not answered at all, nothing is sent. You can change your decision at any time with the switch in Profile › Settings › Privacy and data.

What is sent if you allow it:

  • General events: What Firebase collects by itself: the app being opened, time of use, updates and App Store purchases (product, price, currency).
  • Screens: Which screens are opened (for example Today, Stats, Settings).
  • Habits: Creating, editing, archiving, pinning, reordering and deleting. Only the repeat type (for example "daily"), the goal type, whether it has a category, the number of reminders, the data type if it is connected to Apple Health (for example "steps"; never a value) and, when deleted, how many days it existed (as a range).
  • Days: Ticking or unticking a link, rest days and writing a note (not the note itself). Only whether it was today or an earlier day, how it was marked (by tapping, with an amount) and the streak reaching a step such as 7, 30 or 100.
  • Features: The category filter (only whether a built-in or your own category), starting a ready-made program (which one), creating and deleting categories, making and restoring a backup (how many habits, as a range), sharing a story (which template) and the "Done" and "Snooze 10 min" buttons on a reminder.
  • Zincir+: Where the plans screen was opened from, which locked feature was tapped, the plan picked, a purchase starting and how it ended (completed, pending, cancelled, failed), restoring purchases, your answer to the trial notice, and tips.
  • Settings: The value picked when the theme, colour, app icon, first day of the week or language changes; whether quiet hours and the daily summary are on or off.
  • Crash report: the record of the error, the app version, the device model and the iOS version.
  • Firebase's random identifier specific to the app, approximate location (Google may derive it from connection information), and diagnostic information at the moment of the crash.

These events consist only of fixed choices, yes/no answers and numbers rounded into ranges; they contain no text you wrote and no dates. The app does not ask for the advertising identifier or for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency); the data is not used to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Never sent: your habit and category names, your notes, the emojis you pick, your profile name, your email address and phone number, the dates of your days, the numbers of your goals, any value read from Apple Health, your advertising identifier.

Who processes it: The data is processed by Google's Firebase service (Analytics and Crashlytics); this data may be processed on servers outside Türkiye (including in the USA). The Developer has access only to aggregated statistics and crash records. The purpose is to find bugs and improve the app; the data is not used for advertising.

Legal ground and transfer abroad: This processing is based on your explicit consent and starts only when you tap "Share". The transfer of your data to Google's servers outside Türkiye is subject to Article 9 of the KVKK; the Developer is obliged to carry out this transfer in accordance with the Firebase data processing terms agreed with Google and the safeguards required by the legislation in force.

Withdrawal: When you turn the switch off, no new data is sent, the analytics identifier on the device is reset, and crash reports that have not yet been sent are deleted. Data already sent, which does not carry your identity, may remain with Google for Google's retention periods.

Separately, if you turn on "Share with App Developers" in Apple's own settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may also, under its own rules, show the Developer anonymous crash and usage statistics; that is Apple's service and entirely your choice.

When data can leave your device

Your data can leave your device only in these cases, all under your control:

  • iCloud Backup: If iCloud Backup is on, iOS backs up the app's data to your iCloud account and can bring it back on a new device. This is Apple's service under Apple's terms; the Developer cannot access this backup. Manage it in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › iCloud Backup.
  • Backup file (Zincir+): When you tap Profile › Settings › Backup › "Back up now", a file holding your habits and history is created and you save it where you choose (for example iCloud Drive). The file is not encrypted; it is readable text. Keep it somewhere safe and do not share it.
  • Images and reports you share: If you create a story image or a PDF report and share it, only that image or file goes, and only to the app or place you choose.

Notifications and widgets you add to your Home Screen or Lock Screen show your habits' names and streaks on your device's screen. They do not leave the device, but anyone who can see your screen can see them too. You can turn a habit's reminder off or remove a widget at any time.

Zincir+ purchases

Zincir+ (advanced features) is optional. If you buy it, the Apple App Store takes and handles the payment; your card or payment details never reach the Developer. This is subject to Apple's terms and privacy policy.

  • On your device: The app asks the App Store, from your device, what you bought (the product and the purchase status) and verifies the purchase. The day the free trial started and the last purchase status the App Store reported are kept on your device; they are never sent to a server.
  • What the Developer sees: App Store Connect shows the seller aggregated sales reports (such as product, units, country and proceeds); Apple does not give the Developer your name or email address.
  • Legal basis: The establishment and performance of the contract to provide the service you bought (KVKK Art. 5/2-c) and the Developer's legal obligations (KVKK Art. 5/2-ç).

Optional: the automatic link with Apple Health

With Zincir+ you can link a habit to Apple Health: once the day's total reaches the goal, the link is added by itself. The feature is off by default; it works only if you turn it on for a habit and allow it in Apple Health.

  • What is read: Only the daily total of the data type you chose for that habit: step count, sleep time (the night that ends that morning), exercise time or water intake. It is read for today and yesterday, while the app is open.
  • How it is used: The total is used to check whether the day reached the goal and to show today's progress (for example "6,240 / 8,000 steps"); it stays in the device's memory only while the app is open. No health data is written to the app's files or settings, put in backups or exported files, sent to the internet or the Developer. The app writes nothing to Apple Health.
  • What is kept: The habit's setting (which data type and the goal) and the days completed automatically; a completed day becomes part of the habit's history like a day you marked yourself.
  • Permission and withdrawal: iOS asks for each data type on its own screen. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices, and turn the feature off in the habit's editor; nothing is read after that.

Legal basis: Health data is a special category of personal data under Article 6 of the KVKK. This reading relies only on your explicit consent: you turn the feature on for a habit yourself and give the permission in iOS. Because the data never leaves your device, the Developer cannot access it and there is no transfer abroad. Data read from Apple Health is used only for this feature; it is not used for advertising, sale or any other purpose, and it is not stored in iCloud or anywhere else.

The automatic link is not a medical measurement or advice; it relies on data that other apps and devices wrote to Apple Health.

Permissions asked for

  • Notifications: To show reminders and, if you turned it on, the daily summary.
  • Reading Apple Health (steps, sleep, exercise, water): Asked only for the data type you choose, for a habit you turned the automatic link on for; see the Apple Health section above.
  • Adding to Photos: Only when you tap "Save image", to add the story image to Photos. The app cannot see your photos.

The app does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts, tracking (ATT) or the advertising identifier. The backup file uses iOS's own file picker.

Purpose and legal basis of processing

The data on your device is processed only on your device, to give you habit tracking, reminders, statistics and backups; because the Developer cannot access it, no consent is asked for it. Anonymous usage and crash data is processed only with your explicit consent. Health data read from Apple Health is likewise processed only with your explicit consent, only on your device and only for the automatic link. If another feature that collects data, such as accounts, cloud sync or sharing, is added later, this text will be updated and, where needed, you will be told beforehand and asked for your consent.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it or remove the app. A habit you delete stays in the trash (Habits › Trash) with its history for 30 days: until then you can bring it back or delete it for good at once; after 30 days it is deleted for good by itself. Habits in the trash count nowhere and are not part of the backup file. Data read from Apple Health is never stored. Profile › Settings › "Delete all my data" deletes all your habits, history and settings; this also resets your answer to the sharing question. Your iCloud backup and backup files you saved yourself stay in your account or wherever you put them; you need to delete them separately.

Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you can use your rights, such as learning whether your data is processed, correcting it, deleting it, withdrawing consent and moving it, right in the app: correct by editing a habit, delete with "Delete all my data", withdraw consent by turning off the sharing switch or the Apple Health permission, and move your data with iCloud Backup or the backup file (Zincir+). You can also write to bilginapplabs@gmail.com. Your right to apply and complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board is reserved.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
