What does the App do?

The App offers habit and task tracking, reminders, statistics and badges. Its basic features are free; advanced features are offered through the optional Zincir+. The App is provided "as is". Developer: Bilgin Apps (Erhan Bilgin).

Zincir+, the trial and payments

  • Free trial: A 30-day trial starts the first day you open the App; during it the Zincir+ features are on. No payment details are asked for the trial, and the trial never turns into a paid subscription by itself. When the trial ends, the advanced features close; your habits, history and settings are not deleted, and the basic features stay free.
  • Plans: Zincir+ is offered as a monthly or yearly subscription. Current prices, including taxes, are shown in the App from Google Play before you buy.
  • Payment and renewal: Google Play takes the payment. A subscription renews automatically for the same period at the end of each period until you cancel, and is charged to your Google Play account.
  • Cancellation: You can cancel the subscription at any time in Google Play › Subscriptions (in the App: Settings › Zincir+ › Manage in Google Play). When you cancel, Zincir+ stays on until the end of the period you paid for; then you are back on the free version.
  • Refunds and the right of withdrawal: Zincir+ is a service performed instantly in electronic form. By confirming the purchase you agree that the service starts immediately; therefore, under Article 15 of the Turkish Regulation on Distance Contracts, the right of withdrawal cannot be used. Refund requests are made through Google Play under Google Play's refund rules. Your rights under consumer law that cannot be waived are reserved.
  • Price changes: If the subscription price changes, Google Play informs you in advance and asks for your consent where required.
  • Scope: The features included in Zincir+ are listed in the App. We do not narrow the features you bought to your disadvantage during a period you paid for.
  • Tips: A tip is optional, one-time support for the Developer; it unlocks or removes no feature and does not replace Zincir+. Like Zincir+, it is paid and refunded through Google Play under Google Play's rules.
  • Restoring: Your purchase is tied to your Google account; on a new phone it comes back with the same account through Settings › Zincir+ › "Restore purchase".
