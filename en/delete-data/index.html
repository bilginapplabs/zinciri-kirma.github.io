No account; your data is on your phone

Zinciri Kırma is developed by Erhan Bilgin, publishing as Bilgin Apps. The app has no accounts: no sign-up, sign-in, email or password. Your habits, history and notes are never sent to a server; they stay on your phone. So you don't need to ask us to delete them; you delete your data yourself, inside the app, at any time.

Deleting in the app

  1. Open Zinciri Kırma and tap the Profile tab at the bottom.
  2. Tap the gear at the top right to open Settings.
  3. Under Privacy and data, tap Delete all my data.
  4. Confirm with Delete everything.

Deletion happens at once and cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data on the phone.

Stopping sharing

If you allowed anonymous usage and crash data, turn off Share anonymous usage and crash data in the same section. From then on nothing new is sent, the analytics identifier on the phone is reset and crash reports not yet sent are deleted. "Delete all my data" turns this switch off too.

Asking by email

If you can't reach the app (for example, you lost your phone) or something goes wrong, email bilginapplabs@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We hold no account or personal data that identifies you, so we cannot delete the data on your phone remotely; we will answer your request within 30 days.
